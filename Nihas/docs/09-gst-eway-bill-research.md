## 1. Answers to the five questions

### Q1 — Can a third-party SaaS platform legally access e-way bill data for MSME transporters?

**Answer: Yes, but only through a GSP. Not directly.**

Direct NIC API access has hard prerequisites your customers will not meet
([Masters India](https://www.mastersindia.co/blog/eway-bill-api-integration/), corroborated by
search results attributed to NIC criteria):

- **≥1,000 e-way bills per day** generated by the taxpayer/transporter
- Static IP address
- SSL/TLS-enabled domain
- An automated invoice generation system
- ≥200 activities of sandbox testing before production

An MSME transporter doing 10–200 loads/month is **three orders of magnitude** below the 1,000/day
volume gate. Direct access is not available to them, and not available to you on their behalf.

Masters India's own characterisation of the direct route: *"difficult to integrate and might not
be suitable for most of companies."* GSP APIs, by contrast, are *"easily available to taxpayers
and transporters without any prerequisite."*

**⚠️ Confidence: Medium-high on the requirement, medium on the exact numbers.** The 1,000/day and
200-activity figures come from a GSP's blog, not from the official page. The official
`pre-requisites.html` on the NIC portal **would be authoritative and was unreachable** — see §4.
Verify the exact thresholds with your chosen GSP before quoting them to anyone.

### Q2 — Is direct GSTN integration required, or are there approved intermediaries?

**Approved intermediaries exist and are the intended path.** GSPs (GST Suvidha Providers) are
authorised by GSTN to operate middleware between taxpayer systems and the GST portal.

The official on-boarding page ([GSTN E-Way Bill API docs](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/apidocs/on-boarding-process.html))
states the two registration modes verbatim:

> "Once they get the Production access, after logging into the e-way bill system, the tax payer
> or transporter has to select the **'For API'** option under 'Registration' menu to create their
> logins for API access."

> "Taxpayers/Transporters who wish to use the API's through GSP's have to select the
> **'For GSP'** option under 'Registration' menu and select their GSP in the dropdown provided
> and create their own user name and password."

**This is the single most important operational detail in this document:** in the GSP model, the
**taxpayer/transporter registers your GSP themselves, on the government portal, selecting that
GSP from a dropdown.** You cannot do this for them. It is a per-customer onboarding step
requiring their e-way bill portal login.

**Product implication:** every tenant who wants e-way bill integration must complete a manual
government-portal step during onboarding. This is real onboarding friction and belongs in the
PRD as a first-class flow, not an afterthought. It also means e-way bill integration can never
be "on by default" for a new tenant.

The same page also notes the first gate: *"GSP/Taxpayers/Transporter who wish to use API
interface, should get short-listed in the list provide[d] by the GSTN."* What shortlisting
involves is **not defined on that page** — see §4.

### Q3 — Compliance requirements (API keys, authentication, whitelisting)

From official docs and the on-boarding page:

| Requirement | Detail | Source confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Sandbox access | Request by email from a registered email ID to `ewaybill.api.helpdesk@gmail.com` | **High** — official page |
| Test summary report | Must complete testing of "all Indian static APIs" in pre-production and submit results | **High** — official page |
| IP whitelisting | Required to move to production | **High** — official page |
| Client ID / secret | Issued per taxpayer; shareable across multiple places of business under the same PAN, but each needs its own portal username/password | **High** — official page |
| Static IP + SSL domain | For direct access | Medium — GSP source |
| Authentication | OAuth2-style token exchange per GSTIN; tokens are per-GSTIN, not per-platform | Medium |

**Note the helpdesk address is a `gmail.com` address on an official `gov.in` documentation page.**
That is what the page says. It is unusual, and worth confirming through your GSP rather than
emailing sensitive material to it.

**Multi-tenant implication:** authentication is **per-GSTIN**. Your platform must store and
refresh credentials/tokens per tenant. This is a secrets-management requirement, not a config
value — and it means a tenant's GST credentials sit in your system, which raises the stakes on
the isolation model in `04`. Treat GST credentials as the highest-sensitivity data in the
product: separate storage, encrypted at rest with a distinct key, never in application logs,
never readable through any tenant-facing API.

**DLT registration** (mentioned in your question) is a **telecom** requirement for sending SMS
in India, unrelated to GST. It is separately relevant to you — the driver notification channel
in `03` E7 will need DLT-registered templates if you send SMS. **Not researched here.**

### Q4 — Existing providers with API access

Providers found offering e-way bill APIs (all claim GSP or GSP-partner status; **I have not
verified any of these claims against GSTN's official GSP list**):

| Provider | Notes |
|---|---|
| [Masters India](https://www.mastersindia.co/blog/eway-bill-api-integration/) | Explicit e-way bill API product; clearest public documentation of prerequisites |
| [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/gst-suvidha-provider-gsp-e-invoicing-api-access) | Large incumbent; e-invoicing + EWB |
| [Adaequare (uGSP)](https://ugsp.adaequare.com/eway-bill-apis) | Positions as GSP with dedicated EWB APIs |
| [WhiteBooks](https://whitebooks.in/api/e-way-bill-api-for-developers/) | Claims GSP-licensed, developer-oriented, sandbox available |
| [TaxPro GSP](https://www.taxprogsp.co.in/) | **Explicitly offers reseller/ASP channel management** — relevant to your multi-tenant model |
| [Vayana](https://docs.gsp.vayana.com/) | Publishes official EWB spec PDFs |

**Action:** verify GSP status against GSTN's official published GSP list before signing anything.
Several vendors describe themselves as "GSP-certified" or "GSP-partner", which may mean they
resell another entity's GSP licence. That distinction affects your contractual chain and your
liability if they fail.

**On multi-tenancy:** TaxPro advertises reseller/ASP channel management, and PrecisionTech
advertises independent auth/token management per GSTIN — both signals that the
"one platform, many client GSTINs" model is commercially normal. **But no vendor's public
material confirms it is contractually permitted for your specific case.** Ask every shortlisted
GSP this question in writing:

> "We are a multi-tenant SaaS TMS. Our customers are separate legal entities with their own
> GSTINs. Does your licence permit us to make API calls on behalf of many unaffiliated client
> GSTINs under one integration, and what does each client need to do on the e-way bill portal?"

## 2. Verdict on D15

**Not a blocker for a pilot. It is an onboarding-friction and scope problem.**

Reasoning:

1. **You do not need e-way bill API access to run a pilot.** E-way bill generation is the
   *consignor's* statutory obligation, and they already do it today by some means. Your v1 can
   treat the e-way bill number as **a field the Giver types or uploads**, exactly as `03` §6
   already implies with its undesigned `documents` table.
2. **Nothing in R1's core loop needs GSTN.** Assignment, deploy, milestones, POD — none touch it.
3. **The legal risk is not yours in v1.** If you don't generate or modify e-way bills, you are
   storing a reference number your customer supplied. That is a data-handling question, not a
   GST-compliance one.

**Where it does become blocking:** the moment you offer to *generate* e-way bills, or to
*update Part B / vehicle number* when your Mover reassigns a truck. That second one is the trap
— it maps exactly onto your reassignment flow (`03` S14, D10). A truck change mid-trip is a
legal Part-B update, and if your product implies it handled that and it didn't, your customer's
consignment is non-compliant in transit. **Do not build reassignment in a way that suggests
e-way bill sync unless you have actually integrated it.**

**Recommendation:**
- v1: e-way bill number is a manual field. Document explicitly, in the UI, that the platform does
  not generate or update e-way bills.
- Add to `03` §6's debt list: "Part B / vehicle number update on reassignment" as a known gap
  with a compliance consequence.
- Start GSP conversations now anyway — lead times are unknown, and knowing the cost changes
  whether this is ever worth building.

## 3. Product changes this implies

Concrete edits for the other docs, if you accept the above:

1. **`03-prd.md` §6** — add: e-way bill Part B update on truck reassignment is unhandled and has
   a live compliance consequence. Currently the doc doesn't mention it.
2. **`03-prd.md` R1** — add an onboarding story: tenant registers our GSP on the e-way bill
   portal (`For GSP` → select from dropdown). Manual, per-tenant, cannot be automated.
3. **`04-visibility-matrix.md`** — GST credentials/tokens are per-tenant secrets. Add a row:
   readable by **nobody** through any tenant-facing API, including `ORG_OWNER`.
4. **`05-technical-design.md`** — the schema has no table for tenant GST credentials or e-way
   bill references. Both are needed even in the manual-entry v1.
5. **`07-open-decisions.md`** — D15 moves from `[UNKNOWN]` to "not blocking for pilot,
   blocking for EWB generation features". Add a new decision: **do we ever generate e-way
   bills, or stay a system of record?** That's a strategic scope question, not a technical one.
