Product Feedback – Questionnaire
 
1.     After seeing the product – how do you find it; easy to operate or confusing? How does it look? What changes do you feel we could make to make it more user friendly? Was there anything you expected to find, but couldn’t?
2.     What platform/TMS are you currently using? 
3.     Would you consider shifting to this – If no, why not? If yes – why? 
4.     If we charge a fee, would you use this? What are the costs you currently incur on the platform/software being used at the moment? 
5.     Which feature do you find most useful? 
6.     Does the tracking/visibility feature tell you what you'd actually want to know during a trip? What's missing?
7.     Was it easy to find loads relevant to your routes/truck types? What made it easy or hard?
8.     How did the information shown about a load (route, cargo type, expected payment, shipper details) compare to what you'd normally want to know before accepting a load?
9.     Is there anything about a load you'd want to see before committing that wasn't shown?
10. If we collect the funds from the client and then pass it on to you (escrow model), would you be ok with it?
11. What would make you tell another transporter to try this?
12. Anything about your day-to-day work this product doesn't touch at all, that you wish it did?
13. Are you part of any WhatsApp or Facebook groups with other transporters/fleet owners? Roughly how many, and how active are they?
14. Do you think a referral bonus (cash, discount, priority loads) would make you refer this product to other fleet owners?
15. What kind of content would actually get your attention about a new transport app — a short video, a real transporter's testimonial, a text message, a WhatsApp forward, something else?
16.  . Is there a specific WhatsApp/Facebook group, YouTube channel, or local network you think Pulse should be present in to reach transporters like you?
17. If you could pay a small amount to have your load/listing shown higher up or to more transporters, would that interest you? In what situation would you actually use it — every load, only urgent ones, only hard-to-fill routes?
18. Would boosting a load feel like a genuinely useful option to you?
19. What would feel like a fair price for a boost — a flat fee per post, a percentage of the trip value, or something tied to how urgent the load is?
20. Would you want a public profile showing your completed trips, ratings from shippers, years in business, vehicle types — like a track record shippers can see before offering you a load?
21. Would you want a feed showing updates — new loads in your area, other transporters' activity, platform announcements — the way you'd scroll Facebook or WhatsApp updates?
22. Would you want to connect directly with other transporters on the app — to share loads, ask for advice, or refer business to each other?
23. Would you want group/community spaces within the app (like WhatsApp groups, but built into Pulse) — for your region, vehicle type, or route?
24. Would badges, rankings, or recognition (e.g., "top-rated transporter this month," "500 trips completed") make you more likely to use the app regularly?
